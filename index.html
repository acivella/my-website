It was more than 30 years ago that Willie launched his fireworks business. Over time, his fireworks business grew to include three locations, with this one being his pride and joy. I've known Willie most of my life, and over the years, I became not only his partner in this business but was also lucky enough to become his fiancée.

When Willie fell ill at the end of the 2020 season, I stepped in to continue running what he had built here at the original

WILD WILLIE'S DISCOUNT FIREWORKS LLC. His passion for fireworks and the joy this business brought to both of us inspired me to keep it going.

Since 2021, I’ve proudly continued operating WILD WILLIE'S at our 291 and 58 highway location.

I’m incredibly grateful to all of Willie's loyal customers who have stood by us through the years, and I look forward to seeing all of you each season. Thank you for your generous support as we continue to honor Willie's memory.

— Fanny
